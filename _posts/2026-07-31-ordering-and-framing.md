## Framing prep 

The foundation anchors are placed to support the new headers holding up the first and second story floors. 

Those headers will hold joists that hold up the (currently missing) south wall. 

Once the walls are up, we add sheer walls (a thin layer of wood) and the house has a dry shell. 

It's been over a year since it's had a dry shell!
