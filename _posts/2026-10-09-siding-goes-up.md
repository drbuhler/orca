The walls are closed and the outside is finally starting to look like a finished house. Here's where things stand at 498 Orcas.

## Drywall is up

The drywall is hung, and it passed the screw inspection on Monday, October 5.

<figure class="full-width"><img src="/images/posts/2026-10-orcas-bathroom-drywall.jpg" alt="Drywall and moisture-resistant board hung in a bathroom, ready for mud and tape" style="max-width:614px;width:100%;display:block;margin:0 auto;" /></figure>

## Siding starts

This is the part the neighbors will notice. For a long time the outside of this house has been scaffolding, plywood, and house wrap. Now it gets a real skin.

The siding crew got to work this week. Before siding could go on, Logan finished the fascia boards around the top of the house. That was the last of the carpentry.

Half the house is getting siding. The other half gets stucco, which starts next.

## Two crews at once

Inside, mud and tape start right away, alongside the siding. So for the first time we have two crews going: one inside finishing the walls, one outside closing up the exterior.

## Next up

Once the siding is mostly done, the stucco goes on. Inside, after mud and tape, come paint, flooring, cabinets, bathrooms, and tile.

Thanks for following along!
