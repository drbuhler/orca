A big week at 498 Orcas.

**The cabinetry is in.** The custom Sligh cabinets have been installed: light natural wood, slab fronts, nothing fussy. The kitchen finally looks like a kitchen.

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-kitchen-cabinets.jpg" title="Unfinished kitchen with light natural-wood slab cabinets on the walls and a raw plywood island in the foreground" caption="Custom Sligh cabinetry, installed." %}

**The flooring is halfway there.** Custom white oak now runs across the living room toward the fireplace wall, and you can already feel how the room will open up to those windows.

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-living-room-flooring.jpg" title="Living room with pale oak flooring running toward corner windows at night, boxes in the corner, and a fireplace on the right" caption="White oak, halfway in." %}

**We're saying goodbye to the purple doors!** At some point the doors were painted a very dated purple. They're going white, and I'm doing some of that spraying myself. (Yes, that's me in the mask.)

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-purple-door-paint.jpg" title="A man in safety glasses, a white respirator, and coveralls standing beside a door with dark purple paint across the lower half" caption="Goodbye, purple." %}

**Landscaping has begun.** Tiered timber planters by the front steps are filling in with coastal grasses and flowering plants.

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-landscaping.jpg" title="Tiered timber planter boxes by the front steps, planted with ornamental grasses, purple flowers, and orange blooms and edged with rocks" caption="The first planters by the front steps." %}

**Next up: stucco and scaffold removal.** That's when you'll really see a difference. Orlando Santini designed this 1995 quad-level with sharp, angular lines, and once the scaffold comes down, those lines get to speak for themselves.

More next week.
