The outside finally matches the inside.

**The stucco is done.** It's a smooth white finish all the way around, including the carport and side walls. The scaffold is coming down, and for the first time you can see the house Santini drew.

**Light fixtures and trim are going in.** This brass and frosted-glass double sconce sits on a textured, stone-look tile in one of the bathrooms. It's small, but it sets the tone.

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-sconce-tile.jpg" title="Brass double sconce with two frosted-glass shades on textured beige stone-look tile, electrical box open below" caption="Brass, frosted glass, and stone texture." %}

**The pony wall is cut diagonally to match.** The old stair wall ran square against a house that's all angles. We cut it on a diagonal to echo those lines and topped it with a new solid-wood cap. Now the stairs belong to the house.

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-pony-wall-stairs.jpg" title="Unfinished stair treads beside a gray pony wall cut on a diagonal and topped with a light wood cap" caption="The stair pony wall, cut on the diagonal." %}

**The cabinets are painted.** The Sligh cabinets are now white. Here they are with the shelves open and the drawers pulled out, before the countertops go on.

{% include image_full.html imageurl="/images/posts/2026-11-orcas-cabinets-painted.jpg" title="White painted kitchen cabinets with open shelves and drawers pulled out, plywood floor and an unfinished counter" caption="Painted white, waiting on counters." %}
