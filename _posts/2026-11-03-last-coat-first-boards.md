The interior paint is finished. Every room got primer and two coats, and for the first time the whole house reads as one clean, bright space.

With the walls done, the work moved down to the floor. Flooring has begun, and after weeks of bare plywood, the rooms are starting to feel finished underfoot.

In the bathrooms, tile has started in the showers. The backer board we showed last time is now getting covered, one row at a time.

More to come.
