A House With a Story

498 Orchard Street is a one-of-a-kind coastal property in Morro Bay, one half mile from the beach. Originally designed and occupied by local architect Orlando Santini and his wife Sandra, this home is perched atop Panorama Drive, with 360° views of both the ocean and the rolling hills. It has views of Morro Rock. While cooking and grazing cattle while sipping morning coffee, it's the kind of home you only get one chance to call your own.

As of late 2025, it needed work. It was abandoned and slowly fading. We saw a legacy that could be lovingly restored. We took vision and hard work to bring it back to life and make it available for the next generation. This is our journey, follow along!
