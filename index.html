A House With a Story

498 Orcas Street is a one-of-a-kind coastal property in Morro Bay, half a mile from the beach. Originally designed and occupied by local architect Orlando Santini and his wife Sandra, this home sits high above Morro Bay with sweeping views of the ocean, Morro Rock and the rolling hills. Sip your morning coffee while cattle graze on the hills, and cook dinner as the sun sets over the Rock. It's the kind of home you only get one chance to call your own.

In late 2025, it needed work. It had been abandoned and was slowly fading, but we saw a legacy that could be lovingly restored. With vision and hard work, we're bringing it back to life for the next generation. This is our journey. Follow along!
